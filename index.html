02 / OUR ROOTS

Some things
are in our nature.

In the hills of Corinth, among the olive groves of Ancient Tenea, our family has always felt at home.

Knowledge passes from one generation to the next. The hands change; the care remains. Founded by Athina Kordosi, TENÉA brings this family tradition to tables beyond Greece.

Our land's soil and climate nurture the Manaki olive variety. It is here, in the Peloponnese, that our story begins.
